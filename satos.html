SATVISION / MOBILE ENGINEERING

Work that
stays with you.

What happens when the connection disappears?

I worked on offline data handling in the SATOS mobile app, helping people keep their changes and attachments while working without a reliable connection.

Explore the approach

THE CHALLENGE

Work doesn’t
wait for Wi-Fi.

Industrial work can take people away from a dependable network. An app needs to make it possible to keep working and preserve the changes made along the way.

The challenge is to carry those changes, including attachments, through a sync without losing local work. My focus was improving the mobile app’s offline data handling and reliability.

PRESERVE

Save changes together

I used SQLite transactions to keep local changes consistent as they are recorded.

KEEP

Include attachments

Offline edits can include files, so the workflow needs to preserve those attachments alongside the pending work.

RECONNECT

Resume synchronization

When the connection returns, pending updates can be sent to the service and local state kept in sync.

THE RESULT

More reliable
offline work.

The work improved how the app preserves local changes and attachments during synchronization. I also addressed reliability issues in authentication, database migrations, and cached data loading.

The aim was straightforward: make the app more dependable for people doing real work in the field.
